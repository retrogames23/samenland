# src/game rules (part 5: art, rendering, calendar, scenes)
- Replacement building art uses alpha-cropped WebP under sprites/cohesive with a 480-pixel longest edge; registry crop dimensions match real files and night layers share the same crop, preserving scale and aligned windows.
- Occlusion (render.ts): every standing thing — buildings, folk, animals, road crews, carts, traders, messengers, riders, stalls — is drawn in the per-tile depth pass (addProp/roadProp by toTile), never in a later overlay, so nothing ever rises above a roof in front. Ordinary folk and animals may simply vanish behind houses; only key figures (own strolling mayor, hovered/selected villager, robbers, messengers/riders) are re-drawn at the end as a faint sepia "parchment silhouette". Construction props (road works, site piles) sit on the open south verge so they stay visible.
- Regional islands: realm → biome (biome.ts) sets tint/tree mix, and world.ts heightAt picks a per-biome outline and rivers (Europe/lab formula unchanged); ANCHORS lift founded villages to dry lowland and keep rivers away — add a village's coordinates there before ever changing a non-Europe formula. syncGeo resets terrain caches on realm switch; wonder sites of every realm must be mirrored in DB wonder_sites.
- Daylight (sun.ts): sky darkness and window-light onset follow real sunrise/sunset per realm coordinates and zone clock (DST included); work hours, bells and all economy timing stay on fixed clock hours.
- In-game calendar: gameDate/gameYear in time.ts show the realm's real day with the year shifted by YEAR_SHIFT (2026 → 1500); display only, never used for growth.
- Per-tile ground detail (wild turf, field/garden/pasture furrows, plants, wattle, troughs) is drawn from cached variant sprites (turfSprite/agriSprite in render.ts); only textured base fills stay live because patterns are world-anchored. Ground chunks bake at most one per frame — keeps draw calls and panning spikes bounded.
- Flat town dressing (turf, paths, squares, acres) is baked per 16-tile chunk in render.ts (drawFlatChunks) with per-tile tokens checked in pass 1c; at most one ground-or-flat bake per frame, unbaked chunks fall back to live tiles — panning never waits. Figures, trees, sheep and plain houses use absolute setTransform instead of save()/restore() to keep the per-frame command count low.
- Figure anatomy (src/game/figure.ts): the shared leg cycle always keeps LEG_RATIO body height from the hip; hemY only sets how far the torso covers it, floor-length gowns use drawRobeWalk, capes (prince) use drawMantled: cached layers back mantle -> legs -> torso (soft hem fade, never a hard cut) -> ermine facings, shadow centred on the feet; all walkers share walkBob; skirts/robes are cut once at their alpha-measured hem (hemEnd) and drawn as one closed piece over stepping shoes whose ankles reach up behind the hem (never horizontal sway strips, which wobbled only the lower body) and full-body walk sheets are centre-registered (frameShift); legged walkers step through the clean 4-frame LEG_CYCLE (legFrame) with per-frame waistband registration, because sheet frames 5/6 repeat strides and jitter; the dev Figure Lab (key P in dev or ?figs) shows one row per figure with every situation it meets in the game (stand, walk, work, carry, bucket, lantern, rain/storm/snow) drawn through the real game paths (drawFolk, drawCourtWalk, drawHerald), so guides match the game; every figure's walk sprite is its reference look and all its other sheets must match it, and any new situation gets a lab card.
- Walk cadence: court walkers use COURT_STRIDE with whole gait cycles per leg (rest with closed feet), a 0.1-tile arrival deadzone and facing hysteresis in court.ts loop; leg frames are drawn whole (never split at the crotch), the painted cycle carries the foot lift; torso and legs always share one bob.
- Prince's courier: once a seed has an audience row, decrees are fetched/handed in via princely_audience from the own village (HERALD in lang/edicts.ts, heraldDue in Game.tsx); step-flavoured texts in edictText, kinds unchanged — no DB change.
- Prince's messenger: heraldVisit (princely.ts) derives arrive/wait/leave from decree rows + clock and render.ts draws it on the village road (setHeraldRows); never stored, every client sees the same rider, only the owner may tap it.
- Planted rare trees (cherry/quince) are spared in render.ts via sparePlanted: fields/gardens/pastures/hay/fences are not drawn on their tile; display only, structureAt and household capacity unchanged so taxes stay identical.
- Carp pond: a fishery placed away from water derives a pond on a neighbour tile (pondTiles in world.ts); render suppresses structures there — never stored. drawPond draws the 2:1 pond art exactly one tile wide and cross-fades the aligned pond-winter.webp (ice, snow) by season — keep both files the same crop.
- Builders' night rest: bought builds and monuments progress on workClock (time.ts: WORK_START–WORK_END realm hours at WORK_RATE, still at night, equal to real time per whole day); every finish time goes through specialDone/workDone, never `at + specialBuild` — so the shop, mails and chronicle agree and daily balance stays unchanged.
- Town bell: render emits a "bell" sound source for seat3/townhall/belfry/church/cathedral (so from Kleinstadt on); audio.ts rings morning/midday/evening peals and day-time hour strikes only.
- Town-life questions (lang/city-life.ts: brot…seuche) join the same weekly commons slot gated by townRank passed into pendingAll (Kleinstadt, Handelsstadt, Hansestadt; coast via onCoast) — rank is client-side eligibility only, DB encounter_guard mirrors prices.
- Allmende + village-life questions (commons.ts, texts lang/village-life.ts with real villagers filled into {a}/{b}; seasons via lifeSeason, map scenes via LIFE_SCENES/lifeScenes in people.ts): hutewald/backtag/steg plus 14 village-life keys (texts lang/village-life.ts + village-life2.ts) are encounter rows answered at most every COMMONS_DAYS from COMMONS_FROM_H on, outside the visitor draw, never repeating one of the last COMMONS_RECENT; eligibility comes from terrain, and pigs, the bake-day queue and the footbridge (built only in working hours) are derived in townOf. DB encounter_guard mirrors prices and cadence — change both together.
- Seasonal traces (seasonal-props.ts: sheaves, firewood, ice, straw, laundry, wattle, trodden snow paths), workshop cooper/loom scenes (crafts.ts) and the bell pause (pealPause in time.ts) derive from tile hash, calendar and realm clock in render.ts — never stored, so all clients agree.
- Three-field rotation (rotation.ts): acre Zelge + UTC year give winter/summer/fallow and the tile look; display only — yields, household capacity and sheaf counts' economics unchanged.
- Community buildings (backhaus/herberge/beginen/badestube — bathhouse from Kleinstadt, Saturday bath groups derived in people.ts via BATH_DAY): ITEMS + ITEM_STAGE + purchase_guard (once per village, 24h/168h); effects derived — BACKHAUS_GRAIN in goods.ts, HERBERGE_BUILD in specialBuild, BEGINEN_SICK in sickRate and new resident-tax receipts only (never legacy DB tax). Follow-ups: bakeCost (commons.ts) makes the bake day free with backhaus, mirrored by DB encounter_guard; beguines get Person.nurse (sick calls replace the housemate carer); HERBERGE_WALZ raises journeyman odds on roads touching a herberge — all derived from purchase rows.
- Weather (weather.ts): pure from realm zone + season + 3-hour UTC slot (?weather=kind overrides for testing), drawn as one screen-space veil in render.ts drawWeather plus smoke wind; display only — never touches growth, yields or money, so nothing is mirrored in the DB.
- Weather life (weather.ts wetness/shelters): rain moves outdoor leisure/lunch/play home in poseOf, takes laundry in, adds hoods/lean/breath, storm-lit workshops and weather banter — display only, nothing stored.
- Whole-body walk sheets: every walking figure gets its own painted 4-step sheet (`<name>-walk4.webp`, frames contact/passing/contact/passing, alpha-cropped, shared ground line, torso-centred) registered in WALKS/WALK4 (folk-sprites.ts); standing uses its passing frame unless STANDS names a closed-feet picture of the same body (mason, porter). The torso + shared leg-sheet composite (drawLegged, HIPS, keep boxes) is only a fallback for figures not yet converted, because pasting one leg cycle under differently painted torsos never matches proportions.
- Walk-sheet gate: every new or replaced walk sheet (people and animals) must pass scripts/check-walk-sheets.py (stride, ground line, centring, constant torso) and src/game/walk.test.ts before registration; KNOWN_BAD in that test only shrinks. Process and fault table: docs/walk-animation-guide.md — image generators alone cannot keep a gait consistent.
- 2×2 ensembles (FOOT2 in world.ts: Beginenhof, university, cloister garden, cathedral): sprite on the front tile, three back tiles become paved square in specialSites — keeps scale to figures; display/placement only, prices unchanged.
- House variants (building-sprites.ts `variants`): farmhouse and cottage carry VARIANT_COUNT paintings per style (grain/workshop/gateway); variantSlot(tile hash) picks one, slot 0 = original art, empty slots fall back to it. Variants keep the base kind's width/base and need their own aligned night layer — purely visual, households and taxes use the kind.
- Ground contact: paintings without painted ground get a soft contact shadow from their own silhouette (drawGroundShadow in building-sprites.ts); paintings that carry their own ground are listed in GROUNDED there — add every new grounded painting to it.
- Mills: watermill/mill day sprites contain no wheel or sails — the animated wheel (overshot, clockwise, plane on the pit wall) and sails in building-sprites.ts animate() are the only moving parts; replaced originals stay under sprites/cohesive/legacy/ so each swap can be undone.
- Winter ways (waySnow in seasonal-props.ts: frost / light / heavy snow from season + weather): render.ts draws dirt tracks via winterTrack, streets/squares with snow dabs, trade-road ribbons with broken ruts, all using the barely-snowed "track" texture role; the level is part of the flat-chunk bake key — display only, geometry and widths unchanged.
- Night masks: every day painting declares its mask beside it (`night` in sprites/styled/variants of building-sprites.ts, null = deliberately unlit; nightPairs() lists all); night-masks.test.ts audits size/crop/alpha/silhouette and light areas (hints only) against fingerprints in night-masks.lock.json — after changing a day painting, review the pair in the Figure-Lab "Nachtmasken" card and re-approve via scripts/night-masks-approve.ts; masks are derived from glass panes with scripts/derive-night-mask.py, one file at a time, never in bulk.
- Night-mask lock entries keep three independent dimensions (technical / visual / decision: open, approved, rework, unlit); only a person sets visual+decision via scripts/night-masks-approve.ts, and scripts/night-mask-sheets.py only prints contact sheets and hints for open masks — so a passing audit never counts as a visual approval.
- Ground vs vegetation (grass/meadow/pasture first): ground textures show only ground material (fine turf, earth, moss), seamless, without perspective or single plants; every standing plant is a separately placed sprite with a foot anchor, upper-left light and a renderer contact shadow, sized in world px against the 5-px villager and the sheep. Sizes/frequencies live only in VEG (render.ts); textureAlt stays a weak overlay (VEG.altAlpha). The dev Figure-Lab "Boden" tab (drawGroundLab) shows the reference pasture — check it after every ground change.
- Terrain textures follow the forest-floor pilot standard (docs/terrain/waldboden-pilot/README.md): back up the old file first, plain material with no painted single plants, scripts/terrain_qa.py gives hints only and never replaces visual approval of 3×3/5×5 tilings and in-game shots — one terrain type per work package.
- Ploughed acres, gardens and the fertile floodplain use separate textures (fertileAcker, fertileGarten, fertile in nature-sprites.ts) so each soil can be tuned alone; all share SPAN 70.
